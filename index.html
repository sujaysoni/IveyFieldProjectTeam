The Mission

A consulting engagement built for strategic relevance.

The Ivey Field Project is the cornerstone of experiential learning at Ivey because it places a six-person team inside a real client context, where the work must withstand executive scrutiny and lead to actionable recommendations.

Executive summary

The IFP is a cross-enterprise consulting experience in which students partner with an organization to study an issue, problem, or opportunity of operational, organizational, or strategic significance. The work culminates in evidence-based recommendations and an implementation-oriented final report for senior management.

  • Focus on innovation, disruption, large-scale change, and strategic decision-making.
  • Blend internal diagnostics with external market, industry, and competitor insights.
  • Translate analysis into a practical plan for action rather than an academic summary.

What is IFP?

The cornerstone of the Ivey experience.

The public Ivey materials consistently position IFP as a professional consulting relationship, not a classroom simulation. That means the language on this page should emphasize significance, multidisciplinary analysis, confidentiality, professionalism, and implementation readiness.

Strategic scope

IFP projects are intended to address issues with organization-wide implications, including expansion choices, strategy reassessment, operational redesign, market opportunities, and other enterprise-level challenges.

Senior guidance

Each team works with a faculty supervisor and an Executive-in-Residence who pressure-test assumptions, methodologies, and the practicality of the final recommendations.

Client impact

The end product is a professional final report and presentation intended to help the client's leadership team move forward with clearer evidence and a stronger plan for action.

Methodology

How we approach the work.

This section mirrors the language a client would expect from a professional consulting team: rigorous discovery, evidence-based analysis, and implementation-oriented thinking.

Primary Research

Interviews, stakeholder conversations, and issue framing help us surface the realities behind the brief and identify what matters most to decision-makers.

Secondary Insights

Industry structure, market dynamics, competitor moves, and operating benchmarks provide the external context needed to test the client's options.

Actionable Implementation

We aim to move beyond diagnosis by defining practical recommendations, sequencing priorities, and clarifying the path from insight to execution.

Strategic Value

What we bring to the client.

As Ivey AMBA consultants, the value proposition is not just smart thinking. It is disciplined, cross-functional analysis delivered in a format senior stakeholders can use.

Evidence-based analysis

We combine data, market evidence, and internal context to ensure recommendations are grounded in fact rather than intuition alone.

Cross-enterprise perspective

IFP teams are expected to integrate insights across functions, which is essential when the client challenge spans operations, growth, people, and strategy.

Implementation focus

We frame outputs with decision usefulness in mind, prioritizing clarity, practicality, and the actions required to move from recommendation to results.
