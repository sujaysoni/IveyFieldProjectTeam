The Mission

A consulting project built for managing disruption.

The Ivey Field Project focuses on issues of managing innovation, disruption, and large-scale change that may originate outside or inside the organization. The shock need not be technological but may include issues related to demographics, public policy, consumer preferences, labour practices, or novel business models.

What is IFP?

The cornerstone of the Ivey experience.

The IFP is a professional consulting relationship — not a classroom simulation. Each engagement is built around significance, multidisciplinary analysis, and implementation readiness, with the client's senior leadership as the primary audience.

Client Commitment

The ongoing time commitment will vary depending on key deliverables (July, September, October) to help the team get up to speed on the company.

Access to Information

To assess challenges and opportunities, the client will need to provide access to financial and other information in a timely manner.

Confidentiality

The IFP faculty, student team, and staff will keep the client report confidential and will not release information or provide access to third parties without first receiving written permission from the client, producing deliverables with tangible benefits for your organization.

Methodology

How we approach the work.

Our process mirrors what a client would expect from a professional consulting team: rigorous discovery, evidence-based analysis, and implementation-oriented thinking at every stage.

Primary Research

Interviews, stakeholder conversations, and issue framing help us surface the realities behind the brief and identify what matters most to decision-makers.

Secondary Insights

Industry structure, market dynamics, competitor moves, and operating benchmarks provide the external context needed to test the client’s options.

Actionable Implementation

We move beyond diagnosis by defining practical recommendations, sequencing priorities, and clarifying the path from insight to execution.

Project Timeline

What to expect, and when.

Our engagement runs from May to October 2026 across three structured phases. Click any phase to expand its details.

1
Phase 1 — May → Jul 22

Scoping & Research Strategy

Due: July 22, 2026
  • Define the central business problem & strategic opportunity
  • Set SMART objectives aligned to your organization’s priorities
  • Build an issue tree breaking the problem into workable components
  • Design primary & secondary research plan
  • Submit scoping document; review with Faculty Supervisor & EIR
Your role as client Introductory meeting to align on scope; provide access to key contacts and background materials to help the team get up to speed quickly.
2
Phase 2 — Jul 22 → Sep 1

Research & Interim Report

Due: September 1, 2026
  • Conduct stakeholder interviews & industry analysis
  • Gather & synthesize primary and secondary research
  • Surface preliminary conclusions & early insights
  • Flag any scope changes or risks to the work plan
  • Submit interim status report to Faculty Supervisor & EIR
Your role as client Interim check-in meeting to share feedback on preliminary findings and confirm research direction before the final push.
3
Phase 3 — Sep 1 → Oct 26

Final Deck & Presentation

Due: October 26, 2026
  • Finalize analysis & build evidence-based recommendations
  • Develop a detailed, sequenced implementation roadmap
  • Prepare executive slide deck (max 25 slides) & written report
  • Pre-presentation review with Faculty Supervisor & EIR
  • Live presentation to your senior leadership team
Your role as client Attend the final presentation with your senior leadership team. Provide a brief confirmation letter acknowledging receipt of the completed project.
