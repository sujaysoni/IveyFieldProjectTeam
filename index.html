Our Mission

Built to navigate disruption — and support the people leading through it.

Our engagement focuses on issues of managing innovation, disruption, and large-scale change that may originate outside or inside the organization. The source of change need not be technological — it may stem from demographics, public policy, consumer preferences, labour practices, or novel business models. Our team’s goal is to translate complexity into clear, decision-ready insights that support C-level leaders in choosing and executing a path forward.

Why Choose Us

Frankly? It’s a pretty unique opportunity.

Where else do you get a team of six sharp, experienced MBA professionals — fully dedicated to your strategic challenge, for free? This is a rare chance to access the quality of thinking typically reserved for expensive consulting engagements, backed by world-class faculty and mentored by seasoned executives.

What your organization gains

A genuine consulting experience, not a student exercise.

  • A fresh, unbiased perspective on a significant business issue — whether you’re a blue-chip multinational, a fast-growing venture, or a nonprofit navigating change.
  • Six highly motivated MBA professionals who treat your mandate as their most important priority for the duration of the project.
  • Oversight from an Ivey faculty member and an Executive-in-Residence — a very experienced mentor who has or currently holds C-level responsibilities, with most having deep consulting experience.
  • Exposure to the latest strategic frameworks, tools, and thinking applied directly to your situation.
  • A first-hand look at the strategic capabilities of talented professionals — and a natural pipeline for future talent.
  • A fully confidential engagement, protected by Ivey NDAs crafted specifically for this program to protect your data while empowering us to mobilize the best of the Western community.
Our Approach

Rigorous research. Action and decision-oriented outcomes.

Our process mirrors what a client would expect from a professional consulting team: thorough discovery, evidence-based synthesis, and thinking that is always anchored in what leadership can actually decide and act on.

Primary Research

Stakeholder interviews, expert conversations, and structured issue framing help us surface the realities behind the brief and identify what matters most to decision-makers.

Secondary Insights

Industry structure, market dynamics, competitor moves, and operating benchmarks provide the external context needed to pressure-test your options and identify blind spots.

Action & Decision-Oriented

We move beyond diagnosis to deliver clear recommendations, sequenced priorities, and the rationale your leadership team needs to make confident decisions and move forward.

About the Program

Grounded in over 65 years of field consulting.

The Ivey MBA Field Project is a cornerstone of the Ivey Business School experience — a professional consulting relationship, not a classroom simulation. Each engagement is built around a significant, real strategic challenge, with the client’s senior leadership as the primary audience for all deliverables.

Client Commitment

We ask for your time at three key moments: a scoping meeting, an interim check-in, and the final presentation. Beyond that, access to relevant context and contacts helps us hit the ground running.

Access to Information

To properly assess your challenge, we’ll need timely access to relevant internal information. We treat everything we receive with the same discretion as a retained consulting firm.

Confidentiality

All engagements are governed by Ivey NDAs crafted specifically for the Field Project program. These protect your confidential information fully while empowering the team to mobilize the broader Western University community on your behalf.
